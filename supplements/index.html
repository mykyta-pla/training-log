How I grade evidence

GradeWhat it means here
Strong Multiple RCTs or a solid meta-analysis, consistent direction, in a population resembling mine.
Moderate Real trials, but small, mixed, short, or in a different population.
Weak Mechanism, animal work, or one small study. Kept for a reason I can state, or dropped.
